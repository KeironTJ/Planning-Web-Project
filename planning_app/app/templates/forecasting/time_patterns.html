Financial-week seasonality heatmap

Each value is weekly net GBP as a percentage of that year's median completed, observed, unflagged week. The median uses the whole available year retrospectively, not information available at a forecast cutoff. Compare similar weeks across years; differences can reflect changing prices, coverage or business conditions, not necessarily seasonality.

Colour bands: <50% 50% to <100% 100% to <150% 150%+. Colours use unrounded values; displayed percentages are rounded. F: quality flagged; P: partial; —: unavailable or outside the extract; N: no positive median. Possible shutdowns are labelled in each cell where configured; historical closure dates are incomplete. Week 53 is absent in some financial years.

Lag relationships

Pearson correlations use completed, unflagged observations at the exact calendar lag, including across year boundaries. Lag 52 is 52 weeks, not necessarily the same financial week in a 53-week year. The sensitivity excludes each year's retrospectively identified IQR candidates; it is not a real-time rule. At least three pairs and variation in both series are needed.
